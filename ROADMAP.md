# Gatearr roadmap

Gatearr is in public beta. These are planned directions, not features included in the current release. The list is non-exhaustive, and priorities may evolve with community feedback.

## Memberships and monetization

Give server owners tools to earn money from their media-server offering: monthly paid memberships, a plan builder, and configurable access tiers.

## Aurora theme for the final release

Introduce the Aurora theme at the end of the beta, alongside Gatearr's final stable release.

## Guided media-server setup and integrated reverse proxy

Provide a complete, step-by-step setup assistant for a media server and its applications, including an integrated reverse proxy. The intended layout is Gatearr on the main domain, Emby or Jellyfin at `play.example.com`, and Seerr at `request.example.com`, with clear guidance through the configuration.

## Invitations from members

Let authorized users invite one or a few friends, with invitation limits controlled by the administrator.

## Music support — no ETA

Explore support for music libraries, including Emby music albums and relevant information in application cards. This is planned for a later version, with no estimated delivery date. See [issue #7](https://github.com/HeklaLab/gatearr/issues/7).

## Suggest what comes next

This roadmap is not exhaustive. Share your use case and suggestions through GitHub's [Request a feature](https://github.com/HeklaLab/gatearr/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) form. Bug reports belong in [Report a bug](https://github.com/HeklaLab/gatearr/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
